How to use thunder-daw

Everything below happens inside the app. Nothing to sign up for.

Home · How to use it · Download

Start and stop

The top bar runs the show. Play starts, Stop halts, Reset jumps back to the start. The numbers on the right read bar, beat and tick so you always know where you are.

Song plays your whole playlist. Pat loops one pattern while you edit it. BPM sets the tempo, or tap the Tap button along with a song in your head. Swing loosens the timing for hip-hop feels. Click turns the metronome on.

Drums and steps

Each row in the channel rack is one instrument. The sixteen boxes are sixteenth notes in one bar. Click a box to switch that hit on or off. M mutes the row, S solos it so you hear it alone. The music note button plays the sound once so you can check it.

Melodies in the piano roll

Click a channel name, and the piano roll below shows its notes. Pitch runs up the left side, time runs left to right across one bar.

  • Click empty space to place a note.
  • Drag a note to move it. Drag its right edge to change its length.
  • Right-click a note, or select it and press Delete, to remove it.
  • The strip underneath sets how hard each note hits. Drag the bars up or down.
  • The lane below that draws volume or filter curves across the bar. Click to add a point, drag to move it, right-click to delete it.

The toolbar above the roll holds the helpers: snap to grid, quantize shaky timing, humanize for a played feel, strum chords, build an arpeggio from your notes, stamp a major or minor chord, and shift everything an octave. Keys outside your chosen scale are shaded darker.

Arranging the song

The playlist is a list of bars. Each bar plays one of your eight patterns, or stays silent. Add bars with the button, pick a pattern per bar, and press play in Song mode to hear it all in order. The loop switch repeats the whole thing.

Mixing

Every instrument has volume and pan. Cut trims the brightness, dly and verb send it to the echo and the room sound. The master column sets echo time and how much of each effect lands in the final mix. The green scope shows what is coming out, and the bar next to the transport warns you before it clips.

Your own sounds

The browser lists sample files from your music folder. The play button previews one, the arrow loads it into the selected channel, which then behaves like any other instrument. You can also paste a file path and hit load. WAV, MP3, OGG and FLAC all work.

Saving and sharing

Type a file name and press Save to keep the project, Load to reopen it. The WAV export writes your full arrangement to a mixdown file. The MIDI export writes the notes so you can open them in any other music software.

No sound?

  • Check your speakers and the system volume first.
  • On Linux, make sure an output device is selected in your sound settings.
  • If the app itself built from source has no audio at all, it is usually missing the ALSA headers. Install them and rebuild.
