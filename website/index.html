thunder-daw

A free beat-making app for Windows, Mac and Linux. No account, no cloud, your files stay on your disk.

Home · How to use it · Download

Get it

Linux or Mac, paste this in a terminal:

curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -LsSf https://github.com/canureal/thunder-daw/releases/latest/download/thunder-daw-installer.sh | sh

Windows, paste this in PowerShell:

irm https://github.com/canureal/thunder-daw/releases/latest/download/thunder-daw-installer.ps1 | iex

Then type thunder-daw to start it.

Your first beat in two minutes

  1. Press Play. You hear the demo groove.
  2. In the channel rack, click the numbered boxes to turn kick, snare and hat steps on and off.
  3. Click a channel name, then draw notes in the piano roll. Click empty space to add a note, drag one to move it, pull its right edge to make it longer.
  4. Open the playlist and line up patterns bar by bar to build a song.
  5. Press Export next to WAV to save your mix as a file you can send anyone.

What is inside

Drumskick, snare, clap and hats ready to go, or load your own samples.
Bass, lead, padssynth sounds you shape with cutoff, resonance and envelopes.
Piano rollwrite melodies with the mouse. Scales light up so wrong notes stand out.
Playlisteight patterns, arranged bar by bar into a full song.
Mixervolume, pan and echo/reverb sends per instrument, plus a master section.
Exportsave the mix as WAV or take the notes as a MIDI file into another app.

Good to know

  • One project holds 8 instruments. Save it as a file and reopen it any time.
  • Building from source takes a Rust toolchain. Details are in the repo readme.
